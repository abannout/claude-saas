# Which mobile game to build and pitch to publishers (research, Sep 2026)

**Goal:** €1–2k per month, then scale. Solo dev with a full-time job, small budget, publisher route.

---

## 1. Market map: what's making money right now

**Hybrid-casual puzzle is where the money is.** It was the only casual segment to grow IAP revenue: +20% to $4.2B ([Azur Games 2026 report](https://azurgames.com/blog/hypercasual-and-hybrid-casual-in-2026-full-report/)).

| Game (publisher) | Mechanic | Revenue signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Pixel Flow** (Loom → Scopely, $1B+ acquisition) | Colour shooters on a conveyor reveal pixel art; a planning **tray** | $108M+ in 2026; **23% of all hybrid-casual puzzle revenue** ([Gamigion H1](https://www.gamigion.com/top-10-hybridcasual-puzzles-in-h1-2026/), [2026 releases](https://www.gamigion.com/top-grossing-hybridcasual-games-released-in-2026/)) |
| **Screwdom** (iKame) | 3D screw puzzle | $45.8M+ |
| **Color Block Jam** (Rollic) | Block jam | $44.1M+ in 2026, $114M lifetime ([Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/sort-vs-block-genres-yearly-revenue-comparison/)) |
| **Magic Sort / Knit Out / Yarn Loop** | Sort variants | Sort genre ~$200M/yr across 3 titles ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/one-genre-three-strategies-how-magic-sort-knit-out-and-pixel-flow-are-redefining-sort-puzzle-monetisation/)) |
| **Marble Sort!** (Voodoo) | Sort + **physics** marbles on a circular conveyor | ~$9M IAP in Apr–May 2026, >$5M/month ([AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/voodoo-new-big-three/?hl=en), [Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/marble-sort-crosses-the-5m-monthly-iap-revenue-milestone/)) |
| **Sand Loop** (Voodoo) | Fill buckets with **sand** to "unpaint" a picture (ASMR) | $15M IAP lifetime, 9.5M downloads |
| **Smash Fest!** (Flow Games) | **Physics** cannon smashes jar stacks, limited shots | 14–15M installs and $4M IAP in 2 months; July 2026 breakout #1 ([Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/smash-fest-casual-liveops-turned-into-genre-wide-trend/)) |

### Three patterns behind the hits
1. **A planning tray that creates a monetizable failure.**
   - Pixel Flow's **$5.99 fail offer is ~33% of its IAP revenue** and 34% of first purchases ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/one-genre-three-strategies-how-magic-sort-knit-out-and-pixel-flow-are-redefining-sort-puzzle-monetisation/)).
   - The key design question is *"what kind of puzzle failure creates a monetizable emotion"* ([Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/pixel-flow-and-the-rise-of-sort-puzzles)).
2. **Physics is the 2026 differentiator:** Marble Sort, Sand Loop and Smash Fest.
3. **Mechanics spun out of casual-game LiveOps minigames.** Smash Fest is almost identical to Toon Blast's *Cannon Fest* event minigame ([Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/smash-fest-casual-liveops-turned-into-genre-wide-trend/)). Playrix's pull-the-pin ads also became real gameplay, and **digging minigames are now in "most successful games' LiveOps calendars"** ([Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/engaging-mini-games-and-where-to-find-them/)).

### What NOT to do: the clone graveyard
- **Theme swaps get cloned within months:**
  - H1 2026 saw **137 new "pixel" games, 194 "flow", 215 "hole"** ([Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/sort-vs-block-genres-yearly-revenue-comparison/)).
  - Smash Fest has **38 clones** ([Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/smash-fest-38-clones-5m-revenue-old-mechanic-new-drama/)).
  - Thread/embroidery sort has 5+ games ([Tiny Stitches](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=games.taplab.tinystitches&hl=en), [Thread Sort](https://www.playagame.io/puzzles/thread-sort/), [Wool Art](https://moondropgames.com/game/wool-art-sort-puzzle-3d)).
  - Bead/pixel sort has 5+ ([Pixel Beads](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kiwifun.game.android.pixel.beads), [Gem Sort](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kb.gemsort&hl=en_US)).
  - **Arrow-escape + 7-slot tray** (my earlier "Balloon/Fish Jam" core) already has 5+ games in 2026 ([Arrow Unjam](https://arrowunjam.com/), [Arrow Jam!](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/arrow-jam-tap-puzzle/id6762192889), [Color Arrow Jam](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=puzzle.arrow.jam.escape.gp&hl=en)). **So I'm dropping that idea.**
- **Clones get removed:**
  - Color Blaze Shooter made $8M in 4 months, then was **pulled for being too similar to Pixel Flow**.
  - Colony Flow and Smash Fest were pulled from Google Play; the word "Flow" appears to be protected ([Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/2-top-chart-puzzle-games-are-just-removed/)).
  - **Never copy another game's art, UI or name. Fuse mechanics; don't clone games.**

## 2. The gap: a "digging" spin-out

**Recommendation: "DIG JAM" (working title; check the name is free).** It follows all three hit patterns and I found **no standalone hybrid-casual digging puzzle**. Existing digging games are incremental or simulation titles ([The Digging Game](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.playnation.digging.a.hole), [Lucky Dig](https://puzzle-game.io/game/lucky-dig)) or energy-based adventures ([Diggy's Adventure](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=air.com.pixelfederation.diggy&hl=en_IN)).

**Core loop (10-second readable):**
1. A cross-section of **layered sand and dirt** with coloured gems and relics buried inside.
2. **Tap to dig.** Sand **collapses with falling-sand physics**: chain reactions, and ASMR (autonomous sensory meridian response: the "satisfying" relaxing feel), the proven Sand Loop appeal.
3. Freed gems **slide into a 7-slot tray**. Three of the same colour clear.
4. **Tray full means fail**, which triggers the fail offer (+2 slots / shovel booster for coins, a rewarded ad, or $2.99–5.99).
5. Level goal: **excavate the buried artifact** (a satisfying reveal, like Pixel Flow's picture reveal).

**Meta (IAP depth):** artifacts fill a **museum** you decorate room by room. Collections, events and a digging-themed battle pass. This appeals to the **women 35+ core puzzle payers**: ~48% of women mobile gamers name puzzle as their favourite genre, and they play longer sessions ([YouAppi](https://youappi.com/blog/the-untapped-potential-of-female-mobile-gamers/)).

**Why publishers are likely to like it:**
| What publishers look for | Dig Jam |
|---|---|
| Proven monetization core | ✅ Tray + fail offer (the Pixel Flow model) |
| Fresh but familiar | ✅ Digging is played by millions inside Royal Match/Monopoly Go-style events, but has no standalone hybrid version |
| 2026 trend | ✅ Physics/ASMR (Sand Loop, Marble Sort) |
| Ad creative in 3 seconds (low CPI) | ✅ Dig → sand avalanche → gems pop → tray clears → artifact revealed |
| Depth for D7 and IAP | ✅ Layer types (rock needs 2 hits, water flows, TNT), museum meta |
| Solo-buildable | ✅ Falling sand is a classic cellular-automaton algorithm, which suits a CS background |

**Honest risks:**
- A publisher may see it as close to **Sand Loop** (Voodoo's). Pitch it to Voodoo as complementary, and keep the art distinct (earthy dig site vs. colourful sand art).
- Another studio may launch something similar first. Speed matters, so prototype in 3 weeks.
- Falling-sand physics must run on low-end Android. Use a fixed-grid simulation, not rigid-body physics.
- **The CPI and retention test decides, not this document.** Verify on the stores before building (search "dig jam", "dig sort", "excavation puzzle", "treasure dig puzzle").

### Backup concepts (prototypes #2 and #3)
- **B: a different LiveOps spin-out.** Watch Royal Match, Royal Kingdom (it added *Cube Blast*, inspired by Block Blast), Toon Blast and Monopoly Go event minigames for 2–4 weeks ([Naavik on Royal Match's minigames](https://naavik.co/digest/royal-match-finding-success-through-iteration/)). Pick one **without** a standalone game, and add a tray and physics. This is repeatable and exactly how Smash Fest happened.
- **C: a physics shot game with a colour-sort twist.** Colour-coded shots break only matching jars, and wrong hits fill a tray. It's a fast follow of Smash Fest, but **38 clones already exist**, so it's higher risk. Only do it with a clearly different core and your own art.

## 3. The numbers: what you need to make €1–2k per month

**Benchmarks used:**
- **Retention:**
  - Publisher-ready targets are **D1 ~40%, D7 ~10%, CPI $0.30–0.50** ([StudioKrew/GGA](https://studiokrew.com/blog/hybrid-casual-games-vs-traditional-mobile-games/)).
  - SayGames wants **D1 35%+ with a clear meta** (see earlier sources).
  - The median game has only **22% D1 and <4% D7** ([GGA KPIs](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-mobile-game-kpis-benchmarks-2026/)).
- **Revenue per player:** hybrid-casual **ARPDAU (average revenue per daily active user) is $0.15–0.50**; the median game is ~$0.10 ([Playio](https://blog.playio.co/arpdau-benchmarks-mobile-games), [GGA](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-04-16-hybrid-casual-game-design-strategy-2026/)).
- **Install costs:**
  - The hybrid-casual Android global CPI rose **$0.54 → $0.95** (2024→2025, Adjust) ([GGA](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-04-16-hybrid-casual-game-design-strategy-2026/)).
  - Blended gaming CPI is **$0.56**; US iOS is **$2.37** ([FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/2026-mobile-game-user-acquisition-cost-benchmarks-how-much-should-you-spend/), [megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/cpi-mobile-game-guide/)).
- **Ad eCPMs:** US rewarded video $14–22, US interstitial $9–14, tier-3 blended $2–5 ([RevenueFlex](https://revenueflex.com/blog/app-ad-revenue-benchmarks-2026/)).

**The model:**
- Revenue split is 50/50 ads/IAP, with a 15% store cut on IAP, so you keep 92.5%.
- Retention follows a power-law curve. "Lifetime" means the average number of days a player is active over 180 days.

| Scenario | D1 / D7 | Lifetime (180 d) | ARPDAU | LTV per install (180 d / 30 d) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Publisher-grade** | 40% / 12% | 7.8 days | $0.15 | **$1.08 / $0.56** |
| **Weak** (near median) | 30% / 7% | 4.4 days | $0.10 | **$0.41 / $0.26** |

**Daily active players needed:**
| Monthly net | at ARPDAU $0.10 | at $0.15 | at $0.25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| €1,000 | 360 DAU | 240 | 144 |
| €1,500 | 541 | 360 | 216 |
| €2,000 | 721 | 480 | 288 |

### Route A: self-publish with only organic installs
- Publisher-grade game, €1.5k/month: 360 DAU ÷ 7.8 = **~46 new installs a day (~1,400/month)**.
- Weak game: 541 ÷ 4.4 = **~123 installs a day (~3,700/month)**.
- A new indie puzzle game usually gets **5–30 organic installs a day** without store featuring or a viral clip. **Organic alone is unlikely** unless a clip goes viral.

### Route B: self-publish and buy ads yourself
- **Publisher-grade game, CPI $0.60:** profit per install ≈ $1.08 − $0.60 = $0.48.
  - Netting **€1.5k a month needs ~3,100 paid installs a month, about €1.9k/month in ad spend**.
  - Payback takes ~5–6 weeks (30-day LTV $0.56 < CPI), so you need **~€4–6k in working capital** before it's self-funding.
- **At the average hybrid CPI of $0.95:** profit is only $0.13 per install, so you'd need ~11,500 installs and ~€11k in ad spend a month. **Not viable.**
- **Weak game:** LTV $0.41 is below any realistic CPI. **Every paid install loses money. Don't buy ads.**
- ➡️ **Only buy ads yourself if you see D1 ≥40%, D7 ≥12%, ARPDAU ≥$0.15 and CPI ≤$0.60.**

### Route C: publisher (recommended)
- Typical deals: **~50% of net profit after ad spend**. Homa Jam offered 50% ([Homa](https://www.homagames.com/blog/the-new-homa-jam-offers-participants-a-50-publishing-revenue-share)); CrazyLabs challenges offered **55% of net revenue for a year** ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/79316/55-rev-share-in-the-new-hypercasual-developer-challenge-by-crazylabs/)). Bonus-style deals net out closer to **70/30** in the publisher's favour ([LinkedIn/Jon Hook](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/hypercasual-games-publishing-fundamentals-business-model-jon-hook)).
- **For you to get €1.5k/month at 50%**, the game needs **€3k/month profit**:
  - ~6,300 installs/month (~210/day)
  - ~€3.8k/month of publisher ad spend
- **For €2k:** ~8,300 installs/month and ~€5k in publisher spend.
- Both are **small numbers for a publisher**. A game that passes their KPIs usually gets scaled well beyond this. **The hard part is passing the test, not the €1–2k.**
- **Hit-rate reality:** Voodoo once tested ~1,500 prototypes a year to launch ~4 games. **Plan for 3–6 prototypes before one passes.**

## 5. Plan (about 8–10 hours a week)

| Weeks | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Store and name check ("dig jam", "dig sort", "excavation puzzle"). Paper-prototype 10 levels. Build the falling-sand grid tech spike |
| 2–3 | **Dig Jam prototype:** 15–20 levels, tray + fail state, 1 booster, rewarded-ad hook, GameAnalytics. Distinct earthy art style |
| 4 | Record **3 ad creatives** (15–30 s vertical: dig → avalanche → tray clear → reveal). Optional €150–300 self CPI test |
| 4–5 | **Pitch 3–5 publishers at once** (check exclusivity terms first): **Voodoo** (physics/sand puzzle fit), **Homa**, **SayGames**, **Rollic** (puzzle leader), **CrazyLabs**, **Azur**, **Supersonic**, **Kwalee**. Send the build + creatives + 1-page pitch ([Homa submissions](https://www.homagames.com/homa-lab/submissions-and-creatives), [SayGames publishing](https://say.games/publishing/)) |
| 5–7 | While waiting: start **prototype B** (a LiveOps spin-out) |
| 6–10 | If a publisher CPI test passes (roughly CPI ≤$0.40–0.50, D1 ≥35–40%), iterate with them on meta and retention → soft launch. If it fails, keep the learnings and move to B/C |

**Your pitch should include:**
1. A 20–30 s gameplay video.
2. An Android build with 15+ levels.
3. One page: core loop, meta plan, why it monetizes (tray fail offer), and the market evidence above (digging minigames in top casual LiveOps; physics puzzles trending).

---

## 6. Bottom line
- **Build Dig Jam first:** a trend-aligned fusion that isn't a clone. Pitch it to publishers so they fund the advertising.
- **€1–2k a month is realistic *if* one prototype passes a publisher test.** Passing is the bottleneck, so budget for **3–6 fast prototypes**, not one perfect game.
- **Don't buy ads with your own money** unless your own numbers clear D1 40% / D7 12% / ARPDAU $0.15 / CPI $0.60.

*Research from web search results (Sep 2026); several analytics pages couldn't be opened directly. Treat revenue figures as estimates, and check the stores before building.*
