# Deep research brief: which mobile game to build and pitch (Sep 2026)

## Founder
- Solo developer, computer-science background, has a full-time job (about 8–10 hours a week). Based in Germany.
- Small budget: about €150–600 of his own money. He wants **publishers to fund the ads**.
- Goal: **€1–2k a month first, then scale.**
- Engine: Unity (publisher SDKs are Unity-first). He can write custom systems: physics grids, level generators and solvers.

## What we need
One game (plus 2 backups) that has:
1. **A real gap or growth potential:** a rising segment, an under-served audience, or a proven fun loop that isn't yet available as a standalone mobile game. Evidence: dates, revenue and download numbers, sources.
2. **No direct clone yet**, or no dominant incumbent, verified by several targeted searches (Google Play / App Store / web). List the closest existing games honestly.
3. **A fun core loop**, readable in 10 seconds, with depth for D7 retention.
4. **Hybrid monetization:** in-app purchases (fail offers, boosters, passes, starter packs) **and** ads (rewarded + interstitial).
5. **Publisher appeal:** fits what Voodoo, Rollic, SayGames, Homa, CrazyLabs, Azur, Supersonic, Kwalee and others greenlight. It needs a marketable 3-second ad hook (low CPI) and a credible path to publisher KPIs (D1 ≥35–40%, D7 ≥10%, CPI ≤$0.40–0.50).
6. **Solo-buildable:** a prototype in ≤3–4 weeks of evenings.

## Already known (don't repeat; build on it)
Earlier research is in `/home/user/claude-saas/GAME_PUBLISHER_PLAN.md` and `/home/user/claude-saas/GAME_FINAL_IDEA.md`. Key facts:
- **Hybrid-casual puzzle leads the market.** Examples: Pixel Flow ($108M+ in 2026), Screwdom, Color Block Jam, Magic Sort, Marble Sort, Sand Loop, Smash Fest.
- **Clone waves are brutal and fast.** In H1 2026 there were 137 new "pixel" games, 194 "flow" and 215 "hole" games, and Smash Fest had 38 clones. Color Blaze Shooter, Colony Flow and Smash Fest were removed from stores.
- **Colour-sort variants already exist** for all of these: arrows + tray, dig/sand, thread, beads, magnets, vacuums, luggage, sushi, colour mixing, dominoes.
- **Current candidate: "Leaf Blow"**, a leaf-blower colour-sort puzzle. No direct clone was found in about 10 searches, but it was never checked directly on the stores.
- Google Play, App Store, AppMagic, Naavik and similar sites are **blocked for WebFetch**. Use **WebSearch** instead; search summaries often cite store pages.

## Go beyond colour-sort puzzles
Consider other genres and angles too. For example:
- merge-2 lite
- word and trivia games with a twist
- solitaire/card hybrids
- idle, tycoon and arcade-idle
- "survivor"-lite roguelites
- casual sports and physics
- cozy sims
- games for older adults (brain, memory)
- AI-powered games
- async social or party games
- LiveOps minigames from big casual games that have been spun out as standalone games
- viral Steam, web or Roblox mechanics not yet on mobile
- under-served demographics (women 35+, 50+ players, non-English markets)

Judge everything on **gap + fun + monetization + publisher fit + solo feasibility**.
