## 0. TL;DR: the ten facts that will kill the most ideas

1. **CSRD is gone for the Mittelstand.** Omnibus I (Directive (EU) 2026/470, in force 18 Mar 2026) limits CSRD to companies with >1,000 employees **and** >€450m turnover. About 80% of the original scope is out, leaving roughly 5,000 companies instead of about 50,000. Wave 2 now reports on FY2027, in 2028. CSDDD applies only above 5,000 employees and €1.5bn turnover, and not until July 2029. Germany's LkSG loses its reporting duty, and fines are cut from 13 offences to 4. ESG vendors are consolidating: Greenly+Normative, Diginex buying Plan A, Novisto buying Minimum.
2. **The AI Act high-risk deadline moved.** The Digital Omnibus on AI (Reg. (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026) pushes Annex III high-risk obligations to **2 Dec 2027** and Annex I (embedded) to **2 Aug 2028**. Art. 4 AI literacy is watered down to "take measures". Art. 50 transparency still applies from 2 Aug 2026, with a watermarking grace period to 2 Dec 2026 for systems already on the market.
3. **EUDR slipped a second time**, to 30 Dec 2026 for large and medium operators and 30 Jun 2027 for micro and small. A simplification package on 4 May 2026 claims about 75% lower compliance cost.
4. **Other delays or cuts:** ETS2 moves to 2028. The Heizungsgesetz 65% rule is abolished. The Green Claims Directive is in withdrawal limbo. CBAM exempts 90% of importers through a 50 t threshold. Germany missed the Pay Transparency deadline (7 Jun 2026). The PPWR labelling implementing acts are late, so labelling lands in 2028 or later. The NIS2 amendment (Jan 2026 proposal) relieves about 28,700 entities. The GDPR Art. 30 relief to <750 employees is pending.
5. **What is live and on time:** Art. 50 of the AI Act (2 Aug 2026), the CRA reporting duty (11 Sep 2026), the Data Act access-by-design rule (12 Sep 2026), **EmpCo anti-greenwashing (27 Sep 2026)**, the Product Liability Directive transposition (9 Dec 2026), German e-invoice issuing (2027/2028), the NIS2 BSIG (since 6 Dec 2025, ~29,500 entities), the KRITIS-Dachgesetz (17 Mar 2026), BFSG/EAA (since 28 Jun 2025), EU energy sharing (§42c EnWG, 1 Jun 2026), the €3 duty on low-value parcels (1 Jul 2026), and the Battery Passport (18 Feb 2027).
6. **Crowded, well-funded categories (RED):** GEO/AI-visibility trackers (Profound: $180M at $1.8B, Sept 2026; Peec AI Berlin: $21M A; Adobe bought Semrush for $1.9B; Google Search Console now shows AI Mode and AI Overviews impressions for free), AI notetakers (Granola at $1.5B), AI voice receptionists (Parloa at $3B, plus Vapi, Retell, Synthflow and fonio, and Meta Business Agent free on WhatsApp), AI customer-support agents (Sierra at $15.8B, Decagon at $4.5B), LLM observability (consolidated: Langfuse to ClickHouse, Helicone to Mintlify, Humanloop to Anthropic, Galileo to Cisco), vibe-code security scanners (Lovable Security Checker 2.0, Aikido pentests at $100, Claude Code Security, Vercel Agent, 8+ indie scanners), CMPs, accessibility, NIS2 GRC in DACH, AI companions, GLP-1 trackers, EUDR tools, and DPP platforms.
7. **Platform risk is now a live event, not a hypothesis.** Anthropic's Cowork plugins (30 Jan 2026) wiped about $285B of software market cap in one day. Claude Code Security (20 Feb 2026) took about $15B off cyber stocks. OpenAI shipped ChatGPT Health (Jan 2026), Instant Checkout (expanded Feb 2026) and bank-connected personal finance (May 2026). Google shipped UCP checkout in AI Mode (Jan 2026) and agentic calls to businesses. Meta made its Business Agent free worldwide (Jun 2026). Shopify's agentic storefronts auto-list products in ChatGPT, Copilot and Perplexity.
8. **Two German go-to-market traps:** B2B cold **email** without prior consent is illegal under §7(2) Nr. 2 UWG, with no B2B exception, and draws Abmahnungen. Cold **calls** to businesses need "mutmaßliche Einwilligung". A pitch that says "outbound to Mittelstand" has a legal problem.
9. **Health-data SaaS in Germany needs a BSI C5 Type 2 attestation** (§393 SGB V). This has applied since 1 Jul 2025, and the transition alternatives end 1 Jul 2027. That is a large barrier for a solo student selling to Praxen or Pflege.
10. **SEO and content as a GTM channel has degraded.** Organic CTR falls about 61% when an AI Overview appears, about 60% of Google searches are zero-click, and AI Mode reaches about 93% zero-click. "We'll do content marketing" is weaker in 2026 than in 2022.

**Rule of thumb from 1A/1B:** since 2025, the EU pattern has been to **delay, raise thresholds and exempt SMEs**. ## 4. Kill checklist (test questions per validity criterion)

Scoring for each criterion: **PASS** (evidence given), **WOUND** (plausible but unproven; the defender must fix it in the rebuttal), **KILL** (fatal). One KILL kills the idea. Three or more WOUNDs also kill it.

### C1. Rising, real pain
- **K-1.1** What is the dated driver (law, stat, event)? Check it against Section 1: **has the deadline moved, has the threshold been raised, or is there an SME exemption?** (REG-01...REG-20)
- **K-1.2** Is the legal obligation on the *proposed buyer*, or on someone upstream (the provider rather than the deployer, the importer rather than the retailer, a large company rather than its supplier)?
- **K-1.3** Who enforces, and has anyone been fined or abgemahnt yet? A law without enforcement doesn't open budgets.
- **K-1.4** Is the pain felt **in the next 6-12 months**? A 2028+ pain means a student burns their runway waiting.
- **K-1.5** Is the growth statistic from an independent source, or from a vendor blog or "market research" press-release mill (e.g., "$7.3B GEO market, 34% CAGR")? Vendor stats count at 20%.
- **K-1.6** After the deadline, does demand persist, or is it a one-off spike (registration, first report) followed by churn?

### C2. Immature market
- **K-2.1** Search G2, Capterra, OMR Reviews (DACH), Product Hunt, "best X tools 2026" listicles and the Shopify, DATEV or Atlassian marketplaces. **If there are 10+ tools, or one with >$20M funding, the market is presumed crowded** unless the audience is distinctly unserved.
- **K-2.2** Is there a **DACH vertical incumbent** with channel lock-in (DATEV, Doctolib, Lexware, Plancraft, Hero, Reonic, voize, a PVS or Hausverwaltung ERP)? Why won't it add this in its next release?
- **K-2.3** Is the claimed gap just "incumbents are expensive"? Price alone is not a gap unless the incumbent structurally can't serve that buyer (e.g., its sales-led motion needs €30k+ ACV).
- **K-2.4** How many vibe-coded indie clones launched in the last 6 months? (Search Product Hunt, X, Reddit, IndieHackers.)
- **K-2.5** If there are no competitors at all: is that because **nobody pays** for this? A missing market is not the same as an immature one.

### C3. Specific audience
- **K-3.1** **Name 100 buyers now.** From which list, exactly (Handelsregister/Northdata query, association member directory, Kammer register, BSI/BNetzA lists, Shopify store lists via BuiltWith/StoreLeads, app-store charts, Google Maps category scrape)? Show the query or the URL.
- **K-3.2** Who is the **economic buyer** (signs), who is the user, and who is the blocker (IT, DPO, Betriebsrat)?
- **K-3.3** TAM sanity check: number of reachable buyers × price × realistic conversion (1-3% in year one) ≥ €5-10k MRR within 12-18 months?
- **K-3.4** Is the niche so small that one lost reference customer poisons the well, or so large that it isn't really a niche?

### C4. Solo-buildable in ≤10 weeks
- **K-4.1** Which integrations are mandatory for the MVP to be useful (DATEV, PVS/KIS, TI/gematik, ELSTER, banking PSD2, ERP, telephony SIP)? Does any need **certification or partner approval** (DATEV Marketplace, gematik, C5 Type 2 under §393 SGB V (REG-L15), TISAX, ISO 27001)?
- **K-4.2** **German regulated-profession traps:** does the product give **legal advice** (RDG, the Rechtsdienstleistungsgesetz), **tax advice** (StBerG, reserved for Steuerberater), **medical advice or diagnosis** (MDR software-as-medical-device, HWG advertising limits), or financial advice (KWG, WpIG, GewO §34f)? Does it process data under **§203 StGB** professional secrecy?
- **K-4.3** Does quality depend on data or licences the student can't get (Beck, juris or Otto Schmidt commentaries; industry datasets; paid APIs with enterprise gating)?
- **K-4.4** Does the MVP need real-time voice, telephony or German dialect quality at a level that takes months of tuning?
- **K-4.5** What security or compliance paperwork will the **first** customer demand (AVV/DPA, TOMs, ISO 27001, pen-test report)? Can a solo founder supply it?

### C5. Reachable (GTM)
- **K-5.1** Name the channel concretely: which association (with member count and newsletter or event), which marketplace (and its ranking dynamics), which community (size and activity), which directory?
- **K-5.2** **CAC estimate** for that channel, and payback against price. For SMBs: can it be self-serve (product-led), or does it need demos?
- **K-5.3** **German outbound law:** cold B2B **email** without prior consent violates §7(2) Nr. 2 UWG (no B2B exception; Abmahnung risk). Cold B2B **calls** need a presumed-consent basis (Nr. 1). If the plan is "cold email 500 Mittelständler", it is illegal as stated. Sources: [anwalt.de](https://www.anwalt.de/rechtstipps/b2b-kaltakquise-via-e-mail-telefon-oder-brief-was-ist-rechtlich-erlaubt-275464.html), [CegTec](https://www.cegtec.net/wissen/kaltakquise-abmahnung/).
- **K-5.4** If the plan relies on SEO or content: account for the -61% CTR under AI Overviews and ~60-93% zero-click (PLAT-09).
- **K-5.5** Does the founder have credibility with this buyer? (Students selling to Kliniken, Behörden or CISOs face trust and procurement barriers. Public buyers need a Vergabe process.)
- **K-5.6** Marketplace dependency: can the marketplace owner (Shopify, Atlassian, DATEV) clone the app or demote it?

### C6. Willingness to pay
- **K-6.1** **Which budget line** pays: existing software spend, consultant fees, staff hours, fines or insurance? Name the line item.
- **K-6.2** Show the math: cost of the problem per year versus the price. The ratio should be at least 5-10×.
- **K-6.3** Are buyers **already paying** for something similar? (Good for pain, bad for immaturity. The defender has to thread this needle.)
- **K-6.4** Is non-compliance **cheap enough to ignore** (no fines, rare enforcement, SME exemption)? If so, WTP collapses.
- **K-6.5** Realistic price point versus German SMB price sensitivity and free incumbent tiers (DATEV Copilot free, Meta Business Agent free, GSC free).
- **K-6.6** Churn: SMB SaaS churns **3-7% per month** ([EverHelp](https://www.ever-help.com/blog/saas-retention-rate-benchmarks), [churnfree](https://churnfree.com/blog/b2b-saas-churn-rate-benchmarks/)). Can the founder sell annual prepay? Is the need recurring or one-off (the "compliance project" trap)?

### C7. Not trivially killed
- **K-7.1** Map the idea to PLAT-01...PLAT-24. **Has it already been shipped or announced** by a lab, a hyperscaler, a vertical platform (DATEV, Doctolib, Shopify) or a builder (Lovable, Vercel)?
- **K-7.2** Is the core value **prompt plus UI plus a generic LLM**? If yes, a Cowork plugin, a Claude Skill or a ChatGPT app replicates it. **KILL** unless there is a moat from the platform-risk heuristic above.
- **K-7.3** Which moat applies: system-of-record integration, regulated accountability or certification, proprietary or local data, owned distribution, or network effect? Name at least one that is real in year one.
- **K-7.4** "Why now, and why still in 24 months?" What keeps the gap open after the first funded competitor notices?
- **K-7.5** Does the idea depend on a **platform that is itself volatile** (OpenAI's Agent Builder being sunset, API pricing changes, WhatsApp policy, App Store policy)?

### Quick-kill patterns (instant red flags)
1. **"Compliance tool for SMEs" whose driver is CSRD, CSDDD, LkSG, CBAM, EUDR, AI Act high-risk, Pay Transparency, the Green Claims Directive or GEG 65%.** Check REG-01 to REG-12 first.
2. **"AI [notetaker / receptionist / chatbot / support agent / GEO tracker] for X"** where X is a big horizontal (all SMBs, all agencies). It's in SAT-01/02/03/13.
3. **"Scanner / checker"** (accessibility, security, cookie, AI Act readiness) with no remediation or workflow: free tools and platform features exist.
4. **B2C health or wellness companion**: ChatGPT Health, 300+ companion apps, SB 243, MDR.
5. **Infrastructure for AI agents** (MCP hosting, gateways, observability, agent auth): consolidating into platforms and security vendors.
6. **GTM = "cold email the Mittelstand" or "SEO blog"**: illegal as stated, or a degraded channel.
7. **Health-data cloud SaaS for German Praxen or Pflege**: C5 Type 2 barrier plus Doctolib, voize and Heidi.

## 5. What would make me mark an idea SURVIVING (so I'm not lazily negative)

An idea survives if the defender shows **all** of the following with evidence:
1. **A live, unmoved driver** (e.g., CRA reporting since 11 Sep 2026, EmpCo from 27 Sep 2026, e-invoice issuing in 2027, AI Act Art. 50, energy sharing since 1 Jun 2026, BFSG for *product* sellers, NIS2 for newly in-scope sectors) **or** a clear behaviour or tech shift with independent data.
2. **A buyer segment the funded leaders structurally ignore**, e.g., below their ACV floor or outside their language or vertical, **and** that segment is **not exempted** by the regulation.
3. **A list of 100 named buyers** from a public source, plus a legal channel to reach them (association, marketplace, community, events, warm intros, compliant outbound).
4. **A moat against platform features** (integration, accountability, data or distribution), not "better prompts".
5. **Plausible WTP**: an existing budget line and a price at least 5× below the cost of the problem, sold annually.

**Areas where I expect survivors are most likely** (to be tested, not granted):
- **CRA for small manufacturers and ISVs** (SAT-19): a live deadline, and ONEKEY and Cybellum price out the long tail. Risks: buyer awareness, and a GitHub or Anthropic security feature creep.
- **EmpCo claim substantiation for D2C and Shopify brands** (REG-L05): a fresh law with Abmahnung enforcement. Risks: LLM-trivial core and Shopify app cloning.
- **Vertical workflow tools tied to a German system of record** where no funded player exists (the defender has to find one). Risks: integration access and certification.
- **Energy sharing (§42c EnWG) operations for small Energiegemeinschaften and landlords**: new since June 2026. Risks: meter and DSO data access, and early-market WTP.
- **B2B workflows that the new platforms *create*** (e.g., merchants needing to manage UCP/ACP catalog quality, or businesses answering AI-agent phone calls from Google). Risks: the platforms themselves solve it.
