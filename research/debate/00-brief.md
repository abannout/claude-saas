# Debate Brief: SaaS Opportunity Research (as of September 2026)

## The founder
- Computer science student ("Informatikstudent"), most likely based in Germany / DACH, so EU and German-speaking markets are a home advantage. Global markets are allowed too.
- Solo or tiny team, little capital. Can write code well. Can ship an MVP in roughly 4-10 weeks.
- Wants B2B **or** B2C SaaS for a **specific audience**.
- Explicitly does NOT want generic ideas. Examples of what they reject: "software for construction workers", "receipt scanning", generic CRM, generic invoicing, generic AI writing assistant, generic chatbot builder, generic todo/notes, generic social media scheduler.

## What we are looking for
Market gaps, new trends, and **newly emerging problems (2025-2026) whose market is still immature**: no dominant player yet, incumbents are absent or a poor fit, the pain is growing, and the timing is right *now*.

Good sources of "new problems":
- Regulation with deadlines that are live now or coming soon (EU and elsewhere)
- Tech shifts: AI agents, MCP, LLM search replacing Google, vibe-coded apps, deepfakes, and so on
- Behavior or demographic shifts: aging, new drugs like GLP-1, new work patterns, energy transition
- Platform shifts: new marketplaces, APIs, and ecosystems opening up

## Validity criteria (an idea is VALID only if it passes all of them)
1. **Rising, real pain**: concrete evidence it is new or growing (a date, a statistic, a law, a trend), with sources.
2. **Immature market**: no dominant solution. Name the existing competitors and explain why they don't cover this audience well.
3. **Specific audience**: a named niche you could list 100 potential customers from.
4. **Solo-buildable**: a credible MVP in 10 weeks or less for one CS student, with no need for huge datasets, hardware, or licenses up front.
5. **Reachable**: a concrete go-to-market channel, such as a community, association, directory, marketplace, or outbound list.
6. **Willingness to pay**: budget exists and the cost of the problem clearly exceeds the price. Give a realistic price point.
7. **Not trivially killed**: won't be wiped out by a single feature release from OpenAI, Google, Shopify, Microsoft, and the like, or there is a clear reason to move now anyway.
