# Shovel ideas: highest chance of first revenue (quick research, 25 Sep 2026)

**The honest rule:** no brand-new SaaS rates 8/10 on chance of success. The best idea in the 6-round debate scored 5.
What *can* reach about 8/10 on the **first €1k** is **"service first, software later"**, sold to people who are already making money in a gold rush. Paying customers come first. Then you automate what you keep repeating, and that becomes the SaaS.

---

## A. Vibe-Code Rescue → "ShipReady" retainer (first €1k: ~8/10 · scale: 5, rising to 7 if productized)

**The shovel:** founders build apps with Lovable, Bolt, Replit or Cursor to make money, and the apps break when they reach real users.

- **Demand is real and growing:**
  - Lovable was valued at $13.3B in August 2026.
  - Veracode found **AI-generated code introduces a vulnerability in ~45% of cases**, mostly in authorization, secrets and payments.
  - Agencies ([Wavect](https://wavect.io/services/vibe-coding-rescue/), [AppCorp](https://www.appcorp.org/specialized/rescue-vibe-coded-app)) and [Upwork freelancers](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/development-it-vibe-coding-lovable-dev-bolt-new-base44-and-replit-ai-app-bugs-1978068229056061377) already sell this. That's a fragmented market with no dominant player.
- **Why it fits you:** it's pure CS skill (auth, database security rules, Stripe, deployment). You can start this week.
- **The offer:**
  1. A fixed-price **"Production Audit + Fix"** (€300–900).
  2. Then a **€99–299/month "ShipReady" retainer**: monitoring, backups, dependency and security updates, and "ask a dev" support.
  3. Later, productize the checklist and monitoring into a SaaS.
- **Inbound (no cold outreach):**
  - Answer public "my Lovable app broke" posts on r/lovable, r/vibecoding, r/SideProject and the builders' Discords. Answering a public help request is inbound.
  - Post anonymized before/after fixes, but **only with the client's consent**.
  - Publish a free "Is my vibe-coded app production-ready?" checklist as your SEO page.
- **Legal:** only test apps whose owner has given you written permission. Probing other people's apps can be a crime under §202a StGB.
- **Risk:** it's service work, so it doesn't scale by itself. The retainer and the productized SaaS are the way to scale.

## B. TikTok Shop Europe: affiliate operations for brands, DACH-first (first €1k: ~7/10 · scale: 7)

**The shovel:** brands and creators making money on the **fastest-growing commerce channel in Europe**.

- **Numbers:** [€500M revenue in Q2 2026 across FR, DE, ES and IT, with 69.9% of it driven by affiliate creators](https://blog.lengow.com/tiktok-shop-europe-q2-2026-e500m-across-four-markets/). Sellers pay creators 10–20% commission.
- **The gap:**
  - The tooling is US-first ([Euka](https://euka.ai/) and others).
  - German agencies are only now appearing ([MediaLabs](https://medialabs-co.com/blog/best-tiktok-shop-agencies-2026) is a Top Partner in Germany for Q1 2026).
  - German-language affiliate operations for small DACH brands are thin. This is unverified; check it in week 1.
- **The offer:**
  1. **Service first:** manage the creator affiliate program for 3–5 DACH brands. That means inviting creators (done inside TikTok's own affiliate system, not cold email), handling samples, commissions and reporting. Charge a monthly fee plus a revenue share.
  2. **Then SaaS:** automate what you keep repeating (creator matching, sample tracking, a German-language dashboard).
- **Content: exactly your format.** *"Top 10 TikTok Shop products in Germany this week"*, *"This €19 product made €40k on TikTok Shop DE"*. Screenshot rankings and post them daily. Brands will come to you.
- **Risks:**
  - Kalodata and FastMoss may already cover DE data, so compete on German-language operations, not data.
  - TikTok's regulatory risk in the EU.

## C. Update on the #1 idea (AI Local Index): the tool market is crowded, so sell the service instead (~6/10)

- The fast check found cheap white-label AI-visibility tools: [Ayzeo from $149 for 3 clients](https://ayzeo.com/agencies), [LLM Pulse from €49](https://llmpulse.ai/blog/white-label-ai-seo-software/), and SE Ranking.
- **Agencies resell this at $500–1,500 per client per month.** The money is in the *service*, not in selling another tool.
- The higher-chance play is to use a cheap tool, publish the German public index as content, and sell **"KI-Sichtbarkeit" setup + monitoring to local businesses** yourself (inbound via the index pages).

## D. OnboardingLab (a "RevenueCat for onboarding flows"): verdict KILL as a standalone SaaS, salvage it as part of A

**The idea:** A/B test onboarding from install until the user registers or hits the paywall, with no paywall product of its own.

**Why it's killed (fatal-competition rules a + b):**
- **[Adapty](https://adapty.io/onboarding-builder/)** already has a no-code onboarding builder with A/B tests for iOS, Android, Flutter, React Native and Unity. On **1 Oct 2026** it [merges onboarding and paywall into one "Flow Builder"](https://adapty.io/docs/paywall-onboarding-builder-deprecation).
- **[Superwall](https://superwall.com/)** already lets teams test onboarding, paywalls and pricing without a new app build, on subscription infrastructure that's free at any scale.
- Indie tools exist too: [Onboardsy](https://www.onboardsy.com/) and [OnboardSync](https://www.onboardsync.com/). [Firebase Remote Config A/B testing](https://firebase.google.com/docs/remote-config/abtest-config) is free.
- **The structural problem:** "no paywall" removes the revenue number that makes these tools worth paying for. The market is bundling onboarding *into* the paywall because revenue is the metric apps optimise for.

**Pivots checked:**
- **Activation testing for apps without subscriptions** (fintech, marketplaces, health apps where the goal is registration or KYC). This segment is real, but its buyers are larger teams already served by Appcues, Pendo or Firebase. Chance about 2/10.
- **Web-to-app quiz funnels** before the download. Crowded: FunnelFox, RevenueCat web purchases and others.
- **App Store page testing** (the pre-install part). Apple's built-in product page optimisation plus SplitMetrics already cover it.

**The best salvage:** add it as a **service upsell to idea A.** Many vibe-coded apps ship with no onboarding or paywall setup. Offer "ShipReady + Monetise": you set up RevenueCat or Superwall/Adapty plus an onboarding A/B test for €500–1,500 one-off, with an optional monthly retainer to optimise it. You use their free infrastructure instead of competing with it.
